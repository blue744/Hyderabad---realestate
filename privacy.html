Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 • Website: https://thewaypropindia.com

1. Who We Are

The Way Prop India (thewaypropindia.com) is an independent Indian residential real estate and RERA information directory. We publish verified RERA project timelines, asking prices, bank auction alerts, and official market data for homebuyers.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Sign-In (Optional): If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo to sync your shortlisted properties across devices.
  • Enquiry & Property Submission Forms: When you voluntarily submit an enquiry, site-visit request, or free property listing, we collect the name, phone number, email, and project preferences you enter.
  • Browser Storage & Analytics: Shortlists saved without signing in are stored locally in your browser (localStorage). We use Google Analytics (GA4) and Google AdSense to understand site traffic and display relevant ads.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To save and sync your shortlisted RERA projects.
  • To respond to property, home-loan, or sponsorship enquiries you explicitly submit.
  • To improve city-level project coverage and website performance.

We do not sell your personal contact details to third-party telemarketers or send unsolicited broker spam.

4. Third-Party Services & Cookies

We use Google Analytics, Google AdSense, and Google Firebase Authentication (when enabled). Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

In accordance with applicable Indian data protection standards, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account or enquiry data at any time by emailing contactthewaypropindia@gmail.com.
