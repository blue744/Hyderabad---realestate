Hyderabad Water Shortage Risks 3–6 Month Construction Delays & ₹100–₹200/sq.ft Price Hikes

Hyderabad Published: 2026-11-21 Source: RegNews

Water shortages across Hyderabad—especially in the high-density western residential corridor—are increasing construction expenses for 350 to 400 ongoing projects, according to industry estimates reported by RegNews.

With borewell yields dropping, developers are relying on private water tankers costing around ₹3,000 per load compared with roughly ₹1,000 for borewell water, adding roughly ₹10/sq.ft directly to construction outlays and potentially pushing flat prices up by ₹100 to ₹200/sq.ft.

Builders warn that if shortages persist, projects scheduled for 2027 handovers could face 3 to 6 months of construction delays into 2028 unless treated STP water distribution via HMWSSB green tankers scales up.
