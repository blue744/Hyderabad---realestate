TG-RERA Completion Timeline Extension to November 30, 2026: What Hyderabad Buyers Should Check

Hyderabad Published: 2026-11-16 Source: PropNewz

Following a Union Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs advisory, TG-RERA granted a four-month force majeure extension earlier in 2026, moving completion timelines that fell between 28 February 2026 and 31 July 2026 up to 30 November 2026.

Homebuyers whose project completion date on the TG-RERA portal fell inside that window should check their project's updated portal status and request written handover schedules from the developer.

Separately, TG-RERA also extended the FY 2025-26 Form-7 Annual Audit Report filing deadline for promoters to December 2026, meaning buyers should keep monitoring quarterly construction progress updates.
