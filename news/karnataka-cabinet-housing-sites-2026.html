Karnataka Cabinet Clears 15 Lakh Free Residential Sites & 1 Lakh Houses Under ₹2,241 Cr Plan

Bengaluru Published: 2026-11-21 Source: The Hindu

The Karnataka Cabinet approved the distribution of 15 lakh free residential sites to landless families and construction of 1 lakh houses during 2026–27 at an estimated cost of ₹2,241 crore, according to The Hindu.

Under the Bhooguarantee scheme, the first phase covers 5 lakh sites (₹1,000 crore), with 23,500 acres of government land already identified across 31 districts.

Assistance under the Beneficiary-Led Construction model was also raised from ₹1.20 lakh to ₹2 lakh for general category beneficiaries and from ₹2 lakh to ₹3 lakh for SC/ST beneficiaries.
