About The Way Prop India

Transparent residential real estate data across 6 major Indian cities

Our Mission

The Way Prop India (thewaypropindia.com) helps Indian homebuyers compare residential apartments, luxury villas, and bank auction properties using verifiable public data—without pushy cold calls or broker spam.

What We Track

  • 147+ RERA-Verified Projects: Official registration numbers, possession timelines, asking ₹/sq.ft benchmarks, and corridor comparisons across Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Pune, Chennai, and Delhi-NCR.
  • All-In Cost & EMI Tools: Transparent stamp duty, registration fee, GST, and home loan EMI calculators tailored to Indian states.
  • Market Briefs & Registration Pulse: Curated RERA orders, HMDA auction updates, and monthly government registration statistics with source links.
